# What the sessions cost

This skill is cw's own. It comes with the engine, is not authored in this
repository, and cannot be changed here.

cw prices nothing: it counts tokens, and this skill turns them into money at
prices that are named every time, because prices change and differ by
agreement.

1. Run `cw sessions --json`, adding `cw sessions --json --since <YYYY-MM-DD>`
   or `--until <YYYY-MM-DD>` when the developer names a span; a span is 31
   days at most, the last 31 when none is named. Each session is listed once,
   at its last stop, with its `model` and its `tokens` by kind — `input`,
   `output`, `cacheWrite` and `cacheRead` — and each day under
   `dailySessionSummary`. If no session was kept, say what cw said and stop.
2. Ask the developer, before pricing anything, whether to use prices they type
   in, or the model provider's published prices as they stand today.
   - Typed in: take, for each model listed, a price per million tokens of each
     kind. A kind they give no price for is not assumed to cost what another
     does.
   - Published: read the provider's price list now, from its own published
     page (for Anthropic, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing),
     and note where it was read and when, to the minute. A model's id is
     priced at the row naming that same model and version
     (`claude-opus-5-5` is "Claude Opus 5.5"), and at no other. Where it
     gives more than one price for writing to the cache, use the five-minute
     one and say so.
3. For each session, multiply each kind of its tokens by that kind's price per
   million for its model, and add them up. Sum each day from its sessions, and
   the span from its days.
4. Answer each session's cost and each day's cost, the largest first, and the
   span's total. Name the prices used for each model, and, for published ones,
   where and when they were read.

A session whose model no price is known for is answered with its tokens and
its cost said to be unknown, and is left out of the totals, which say so.
Never guess a price: not from a model of a similar name, not from memory.
